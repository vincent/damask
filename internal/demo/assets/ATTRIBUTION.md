# Demo asset attribution

The photos and video clips under `photos/` and `videos/` are real, licensed
files curated for the demo workspace (see `../../../ROADMAP.79.demo-real-assets.md`).
None of these licenses legally require attribution, but crediting the
photographers/filmmakers is good practice, so it's kept here.

All files were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, re-encoded/cropped to
reasonable web dimensions, and (for the JPEGs under `photos/`) had synthetic
EXIF written in — see `tools/demo-assets/`.
